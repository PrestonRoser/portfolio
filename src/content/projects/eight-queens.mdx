A solver for the Eight Queens puzzle, written for Data Structures and Algorithms.

- Each candidate is a permutation of the eight columns, so no two queens can share a row or a
  column by construction.
- The diagonal check compares every pair of queens and rejects the arrangement when the distance
  between their rows matches the distance between their columns.
- Prints each solution it finds as a board of dots and queens.
- The recursive permutation solver came with the assignment; the diagonal test and the output are
  mine.
