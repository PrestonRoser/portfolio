A six-page rebuild of an electrical contractor's website, currently live as a preview while the
client reviews it.

- Dark theme sampled from the company's existing branding, with the brand red lightened for body
  text so it passes contrast at small sizes.
- A review carousel that quotes customer reviews verbatim, at the honest 4.6 rating rather than a
  rounded-up one.
- Deployed on Cloudflare Workers, where merging to `main` is the deploy.
